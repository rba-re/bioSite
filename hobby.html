Hobbies

I’ve always enjoyed working with my hands and staying active, which naturally led me to a range of hobbies. Wrestling gave me a strong foundation in discipline and endurance, and since then, I’ve shifted my focus to strength training and performance-based fitness. I'm especially drawn to training that combines functionality with challenge—like tactical workouts that mirror real-life demands. Outside the gym, I spend time working on my Mustang. What started as a side project has grown into something more meaningful. It’s a chance to slow down, troubleshoot, and take pride in progress you can actually see and hear every time the engine starts.

Achievements

In both my professional and personal life, I've tried to say yes to opportunities that push me out of my comfort zone. Becoming a certified self-defense instructor was one of those moments—it allowed me to share useful, confidence-building skills with my fellow corrections corporals. That experience strengthened my leadership abilities and reminded me how rewarding it is to help others grow. I’m also currently serving in the National Guard, where I continue to develop technical skills and prepare for greater responsibility. My next step is pursuing a bachelor’s degree, with the long-term goal of working in defense contracting and contributing to missions that matter.
